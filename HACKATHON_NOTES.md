# Hackathon Submission Notes & Preparation

**Event:** Hacktoberfest 2026 DEV Weekend Challenge — *"Build for a Friend"*  
**Project:** Echoes of an Era  
**Tagline:** *"A living AI time capsule built from my grandfather's voice."*  
**Recipient:** Built for my grandfather  

---

## 1. Challenge Alignment Matrix

| Hackathon Criterion | Project Implementation & Proof Points |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Build for a Friend** | Designed specifically for my grandfather to digitize, index, search, and revisit his oral history while preserving his authentic voice as the sole source of truth. |
| **Open-Source / Open-Weight AI** | Built using open-weight models: **Gemma 3 1B Q4_K_M** (LLM), **BAAI/bge-m3** (1024-dim embeddings), and **faster-whisper** (speech-to-text). Zero dependency on proprietary cloud LLM APIs. |
| **Real User & Problem** | Solves the challenge of unsearchable family voice recordings and protects against AI hallucination by establishing strict retrieval grounding. |
| **Technical Execution** | Complete production-ready architecture: React 18 frontend, FastAPI backend, PostgreSQL 16 with `pgvector` HNSW index, local Docker Model Runner, and PyAV audio range streaming. |
| **Creativity & Innovation** | Features the **"Then vs Now"** historical comparison engine, grounding refusal guardrails, and instant timestamp jump-cutting to original voice recordings. |
| **Open Innovation** | Fully open architecture ensuring family data remains private, local, and accessible indefinitely without vendor lock-in. |

## 2. 2–3 Minute Video Demo Sequence

### Act 1: The Problem & The Human Story (0:00 - 0:30)
1. **Introduction:** State the project name and tagline.
2. **The Problem:** Show a raw audio recording of grandfather. Explain that hours of voice recordings contain incredible history, but are unsearchable, and generic AI chatbots hallucinate fake memories when asked personal questions.
3. **Core Rule:** Highlight the system motto: *"AI interprets the memories. It does not create the memories."*

### Act 2: Audio Ingestion & Extraction (0:30 - 1:00)
4. **Upload Demo:** Show uploading a recording (`.mp3` / `.wav`) via the Home page `AudioUploader`.
5. **Pipeline Execution:** Trigger the processing pipeline (Whisper STT $\to$ Gemma 3 extraction $\to$ BGE-M3 embedding $\to$ pgvector storage).
6. **Extracted Memory:** Display the resulting structured Memory Card showing extracted title, summary, era (e.g., 1950s), verbatim quotes, and tags.

### Act 3: Grounded Q&A & Original Audio Playback (1:00 - 1:45)
7. **Ask Grandfather:** Navigate to `/ask` and submit a factual question: *"How did Grandpa communicate when he was young?"*
8. **Grounded Answer:** Show the retrieved answer generated by Gemma 3 based strictly on retrieved memories.
9. **Verbatim Citation & Timestamps:** Highlight the verbatim quote badge and timestamp button ($12.4\text{s} \to 84.2\text{s}$).
10. **Audio Playback:** Click the timestamp button. The docked `AudioPlayer` instantly streams the original audio starting at $12.4\text{s}$, proving the grandfather's voice is the source.

### Act 4: Zero-Hallucination Guardrail & Then vs Now (1:45 - 2:30)
11. **Refusal Guardrail:** Submit an out-of-archive question: *"What did Grandpa think about cryptocurrency in 2024?"*
12. **Refusal Verification:** Show the system response: *"Grandfather has not spoken about this in his recorded memories."*
13. **Then vs Now:** Navigate to `/compare`. Show a historical comparison contrasting grandfather's 1950s experience with modern realities.

### Act 5: Architecture & Conclusion (2:30 - 3:00)
14. **Architecture Summary:** Briefly present the local stack (Gemma 3 + BGE-M3 + PostgreSQL pgvector).
15. **Closing:** Share grandfather's reaction to hearing his preserved memories.
